Which cleaning service should I choose?

Regular cleaning helps with ongoing upkeep. A deep clean gives more attention to a home that needs an extra refresh. Move-in / move-out cleaning is designed around a change of home. Tell us about your space and we’ll discuss the best fit.

How much will my cleaning cost?

Use the price estimator for an instant estimate based on your home. We’ll confirm the cleaning scope and final price with you before scheduling.

Will I have the same cleaner each time?

Whenever available, we assign a dedicated cleaner to your home. If a trusted backup is needed, they follow the same household notes and preferences.

Do you clean homes outside Cleveland?

We serve Cleveland and surrounding communities. Share your neighborhood or ZIP code so we can confirm coverage and discuss availability.

How do you keep cloths separate?

We use separate, color-coded microfiber cloths for bathrooms, kitchens, and living areas to help prevent cross-contamination between those zones.

What should I share when planning a moving clean?

Tell us the address or ZIP code, your moving date, whether the home will be empty, and any areas that need extra attention. We’ll talk through scope and availability with you.
